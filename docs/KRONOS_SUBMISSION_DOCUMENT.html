1 Problem Statement

Word Count: 198 / 300 Max

Smallholder agriculture in developing economies faces acute systemic inefficiencies that constrain crop yields and drive severe environmental degradation. Farmers currently operate with four structural handicaps:

1. Delayed Diagnostics

Centralized laboratory soil tests take 2 to 4 weeks, returning long after critical crop sowing and nutrient windows have passed.

2. Raw Inactionable Data

Commercial IoT sensors simply dump raw electrical metrics (EC in μS/cm, raw moisture %) without translating them into fertilizer dosages.

3. Blanket Over-Fertilization

Uniform chemical application across uneven fields wastes 30–45% of fertilizers, accelerating groundwater contamination and soil acidification.

4. Late Disease Detection

Foliar pathologies are identified only after macroscopic collapse, causing irreversible yield losses of up to 40%.

Smallholders urgently need an affordable, in-situ diagnostic device that closes the loop between raw soil chemistry, weather forecasting, and localized agricultural action.

2 Designed Solution to the Problem

Word Count: 228 / 300 Max

Project KRONOS is an integrated precision agronomy ecosystem that links portable edge hardware, cloud time-series ingestion, and deterministic multi-modal AI advisory:

• Edge Hardware Diagnostic Unit: A ruggedized field device featuring an ESP32-S3 microcontroller and an industrial 7-in-1 RS-485 Modbus probe. It measures Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), pH, Electrical Conductivity (EC), Moisture, and Temperature simultaneously in 15 seconds.

• Continuous Ingestion & Storage: Data streams to InfluxDB Cloud at 1-second intervals, materialized into daily Parquet and CSV structures for immediate and historical query access.

• Deterministic Agronomy Core: Executes 3-point rolling calibrations, Indian alluvial soil-specific NPK banding, and automated soil texture classification (Sandy/Clayey/Loamy) based on moisture dry-down slopes (dh/dt).

• Weather-Fused Generative AI: Merges live soil status with Open-Meteo 14-day forecasts and ECMWF SEAS5 seasonal outlooks. The payload prompts Llama 3.3 70B (via Groq) to generate exact, crop-specific fertilizer plans (kg/acre in split doses), irrigation advice, and disease risk alerts.

• Spatial Mapping & Vision: Enables single-probe temporal field walks to construct geostatistical nutrient/waterlogging heatmaps (Ordinary Kriging), alongside foliar disease scanning via Computer Vision.

4 Core USP & Key Features of the Solution

USP & Feature Matrix
⭐ Main Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
"Closing the Loop from Soil Chemistry to Field-Ready Prescriptions"

Unlike standard IoT soil probes that merely display raw, confusing numbers, KRONOS is an intelligent digital agronomist. It synthesizes 15-second in-situ 7-parameter soil diagnostics with 14-day hyperlocal weather forecasts to deliver exact metric fertilizer doses (kg/acre in split timings), single-probe nutrient heatmaps, and foliar disease cures in vernacular languages (Bengali, Hindi, English)—at less than 1/10th the cost of commercial lab setups.

Key Solution Features

⚡

15-Second In-Situ Testing

Simultaneously captures N, P, K, pH, EC, moisture, and temperature in 15 seconds without laboratory wait times or chemical reagents.

🎯

Metric-Precision Fertilizer Prescriptions

Translates soil deficiencies into exact chemical and organic application rates (e.g., Urea @ 35 kg/acre in 2 split doses) tailored to crop stages.

🗺️

Single-Probe Spatial Field Mapping

Leverages GPS-tagged temporal walks with a single hardware probe to construct high-resolution Ordinary Kriging nutrient and soil health heatmaps.

💧

Waterlogging & Salinity Early Warning

Identifies standing water pooling versus dry zones by correlating EC and moisture dry-down slopes, preventing root asphyxiation.

🌦️

14-Day Weather-Synchronized Advisory

Integrates rain probability and temperature forecasts to avoid fertilizing before heavy downpours and schedule pre-dawn heat irrigations.

🍃

Vision AI Leaf Disease Diagnostics

Identifies foliar pathogens from smartphone photos and cross-references them with soil moisture to assess disease escalation risks.

🗣️

Multilingual Vernacular Interface

Delivers plain-language, emoji-rich agricultural advisories and conversational guidance in Bengali, Hindi, and English for smallholder accessibility.

6 Impact to Society & Deployment Model

Word Count: 196 / 300 Max

Project KRONOS directly advances United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 2: Zero Hunger, SDG 12: Responsible Production, and SDG 15: Life on Land):

• Economic Prosperity for Smallholders: By replacing blind blanket fertilization with Variable Rate Application (VRA), farmers reduce chemical input costs by 20% to 35% while boosting crop yields by 15% to 25% through optimized macronutrient balance.

• Environmental Rejuvenation: Monitoring in-situ pH and EC prevents secondary soil salinization and toxic nitrate runoff into village water supplies, promoting regenerative agricultural practices.

• Climate Resilience: Fusing irrigation schedules with 14-day rain forecasts mitigates drought stress and prevents crop drowning, conserving millions of liters of freshwater.

• Democratization of Agri-Tech: Commercial precision systems cost upwards of $3,000. KRONOS packages industrial-grade sensing into a sub-$65 BOM with free cloud intelligence, putting digital agronomists into smallholder pockets.
