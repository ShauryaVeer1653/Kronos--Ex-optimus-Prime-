### 💰 Stream 1: Direct Hardware Sales & HaaS (Hardware-as-a-Service)
* **What We Sell:** Portable KRONOS 7-in-1 IoT Probe Kit (ESP32-S3 + OLED + RS-485 Probe + Rugged IP65 Casing + Rechargeable Battery).
* **Cost to Build (COGS):** ~₹2,600 – ₹3,200 ($31 – $38 USD).
* **Retail Selling Price:** ₹4,999 – ₹5,999 ($60 – $72 USD).
* **Gross Margin:** **~45% – 50% per hardware unit**.
* **Target Buyers:** Medium/large progressive farmers, FPO lead farmers, and agri-input shops.

---

### 💰 Stream 2: The "Krishi Mitra" Pay-Per-Scan Franchise (Village Model)
Smallholder farmers (86% of India) cannot afford to buy a ₹5,000 device. KRONOS solves this with a **Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE)** model:
* **The Model:** A local rural youth or agri-graduate buys one KRONOS Kit.
* **The Service:** They walk farm to farm offering soil health testing & plant disease scanning.
* **Pricing:** ₹75 – ₹100 per soil diagnostic report.
* **Revenue Split:** 
  - **80% (₹60–₹80)** kept by the local VLE (earning ₹15,000–₹25,000/month).
  - **20% (₹15–₹20)** platform cloud & AI processing royalty to KRONOS.
* **Why It Works:** Farmers get instant lab-grade soil tests for the price of a cup of tea, and rural youth gain sustainable employment.

---

### 💰 Stream 3: Freemium & AI Advisory Subscription
* **Free Tier:** Live telemetry dashboard, basic NPK status bands, 3-day weather overview.
* **Pro Farmer Subscription (₹149 / month or ₹999 / year):**
  - Unlimited 14-day dynamic irrigation scheduling.
  - 6-month ECMWF seasonal crop planning.
  - Unlimited PhytoScan plant disease diagnostics with Gemini Vision verification.
  - Downloadable PDF Agronomy Dossiers for bank crop loan verification.

### 💰 Stream 4: FPO & Cooperative Fleet Dashboard (B2B SaaS)
* **What We Sell:** Web Dashboard for Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) managing 500–2,000 member farmers.
* **Pricing:** **₹25,000 – ₹60,000 annual subscription** per FPO.
* **Features Provided:**
  - Multi-node spatial mapping across all member plots.
  - Aggregate soil health analytics (tells FPO management exactly how much Urea, DAP, and MOP to order in bulk for the village).
  - Centralized agronomist advisory broadcasting.

---

### 💰 Stream 5: Agri-Input & Crop Insurance Enterprise APIs (B2B / B2G)
* **Fertilizer & Seed Companies:** License KRONOS Soil Engine API to recommend exact seed varieties and fertilizer blends tailored to regional soil chemistry.
* **Crop Insurance Providers:** Verified timestamped sensor telemetry & weather impact data to assess drought, salinity, and waterlogging claims without expensive physical surveyors.

### 🌾 Example 1: Ramesh — Smallholder Paddy Farmer (2 Acres, West Bengal)

```
  BEFORE KRONOS:
  Ramesh spends ₹4,000 on Urea because his local dealer told him "more Urea = greener leaves".
  His soil becomes acidic (pH drops to 5.1), the paddy plants grow weak, lodge (fall over) in
  light wind, and get attacked by Brown Leaf Spot. He loses 30% of his crop.

  WITH KRONOS:
  1. Ramesh gets his soil scanned using the local Krishi Mitra's KRONOS probe.
  2. In 10 seconds, the KRONOS app shows: "🔴 Nitrogen: Excess | ❌ Phosphorus: Low | 🔴 pH: 5.1 (Acidic)".
  3. The AI Chatbot sends a voice note in Bengali: "Ramesh-da, stop Urea for 2 weeks! Apply 35 kg
     Single Super Phosphate (SSP) and 200 kg Agricultural Lime to fix your soil acid."
  4. Ramesh saves ₹2,200 by cancelling his extra Urea purchase and fixes his soil pH.

  RESULT: Paddy stems grow strong, disease disappears, and his harvest yield increases by 18%.
```
